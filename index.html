MEMORY 03

ARCHIVE RECORD // UNKNOWN DATE

It was the day our class collected money for a school activity.

When the money went missing, the teacher searched everyone's bags.

They found it in mine.

“It’s not mine. I didn’t take it.”

But no one believed me.

“Then how did it get into your bag?”

I didn't know.

My classmates whispered about me.
Even my teacher stopped trusting me.

Days later, the truth was discovered.
Someone else had put the money in my bag.
I was innocent.
But no one apologized.

Everyone simply moved on, as if nothing had happened.

That day, I learned something:

Being innocent doesn't mean people will believe you.

So I stopped explaining myself.
If people are going to believe whatever they want anyway…
does the truth really matter?

END OF MEMORY 03
